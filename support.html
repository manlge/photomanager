PhotoManager 支持

PhotoManager 是通过 USB 浏览、导出、备份和整理 iPhone 照片的 macOS 应用。无需注册；照片在本机处理。

设备没有出现或照片无法加载

  1. 确认使用支持数据传输的 USB 线连接 iPhone 和 Mac。
  2. 解锁 iPhone，在手机上确认“信任此电脑”，并处理 macOS 请求的相关访问权限。
  3. 在应用左侧选择设备。大图库首次读取需要时间；保持设备连接并解锁。
  4. 若仍不可用，断开后重新连接、重新选择设备，并记录界面错误。可在“系统设置”的隐私与安全性中检查相关授权。

导出与本地副本

“导出…”不会删除设备原件。导出时选择有写入权限且空间充足的目录。“打开备份目录”用于管理备份；“隐私政策”窗口显示实际缓存与备份目录。卸载应用不保证清除这些文件。

删除与恢复

“删除…”会永久删除设备照片，不进入“最近删除”。“备份并删除”和“导出并删除”在整批复制和校验通过后再启动设备删除。开启 iCloud 照片时，删除可能同步到其他设备。应用不提供自动恢复或写回 iPhone 功能；如已保存副本，可自行从备份或导出目录取用文件。

PhotoManager Support

PhotoManager is a macOS app for browsing, exporting, backing up, and organizing iPhone photos over USB. No account is required; photos are processed locally.

Device or photos not appearing

  1. Use a USB data cable to connect the iPhone to the Mac.
  2. Unlock the iPhone, confirm “Trust This Computer,” and respond to relevant macOS permission prompts.
  3. Select the device in the sidebar. Large libraries take time to load; keep the device connected and unlocked.
  4. If needed, reconnect and select the device again. Record any error shown and check relevant access controls in macOS System Settings.

Exports and local copies

“Export…” does not delete device originals. Choose a writable folder with enough space. Use “Open Backup Folder” to manage backups. The privacy policy window shows actual cache and backup paths. Uninstalling the app does not guarantee removal of these files.

Deletion and recovery

“Delete…” permanently deletes device photos without using Recently Deleted. Back Up & Delete and Export & Delete copy and verify the full batch before starting device deletion. With iCloud Photos, deletion may sync to other devices. The app does not automatically restore files or write them back to the iPhone. Retained copies can be accessed in your backup or export folder.
